## 🖥️ WELCOME TO YOUR PLAYGROUND

</div>

Imagine opening an application because you're bored.

Instead of falling into another endless feed, you're greeted with a tiny world of possibilities. A puzzle to solve. Something to create. A cozy experience to enjoy. A curious challenge to explore. Perhaps even a little adventure waiting outside your screen.

That's the idea behind **SIDE QUEST**.

SIDE QUEST is an open-source digital playground that brings together mini-games, creative activities, relaxing experiences, curiosity challenges, and real-world quests in one playful environment.

It's designed for people who want to make their free time feel a little more interesting, intentional, and imaginative.

Not another social media platform. Not another productivity dashboard. Not another application competing for your attention.

**A place to discover what you could do instead.**

---

## ✧ THE IDEA THAT STARTED IT ALL

<div align="center">

### What if boredom became the beginning of an adventure?

</div>

We live in a world where entertainment is always available, yet sometimes we have no idea what to do with our free time.

We open an app, scroll through an endless stream of content, switch between platforms, and eventually realize that we haven't really done anything.

SIDE QUEST is being built to offer a different possibility.

The purpose is simple:

**To turn passive screen time into active experiences.**

Instead of endlessly consuming content, people can explore small activities that encourage creativity, curiosity, relaxation, discovery, and even interaction with the world beyond their screens.

SIDE QUEST believes that entertainment doesn't always need to be loud, competitive, addictive, or complicated.

Sometimes, a five-minute creative activity is enough. Sometimes, a tiny puzzle is all you need. And sometimes, the best quest is to close your laptop and do something in the real world.

## 💜 BUILT FOR A DIFFERENT KIND OF DIGITAL EXPERIENCE

SIDE QUEST isn't about maximizing screen time.

It's about making the time people choose to spend in the playground worthwhile.

| Our principle | What it means |
|---|---|
| Discovery over scrolling | Curated experiences instead of an endless feed. |
| Participation over consumption | Encourage people to create, solve, explore, and play. |
| Curiosity over competition | Enjoy activities without unnecessary pressure. |
| Freedom over obligation | No forced streaks or manipulative progression. |
| Accessibility over assumptions | Design for different abilities and interaction needs. |
| Privacy over unnecessary data | Keep the experience simple and user-controlled. |

**The objective isn't to make people stay longer. It's to give them something worth doing.**

## ♿ A PLAYGROUND FOR EVERYONE

A playful experience should not exclude people because of how they interact with technology.

Accessibility is an important part of SIDE QUEST's vision.

The project aims to incorporate:

- Keyboard-friendly navigation.
- Screen-reader-compatible interactions.
- Clear visual focus indicators.
- Readable typography and appropriate color contrast.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Touch-friendly controls.
- Alternatives to timed challenges wherever practical.
- Thoughtful experiences for people with different abilities and needs.

Accessibility is not a feature reserved for later. It is part of how the playground should be designed from the beginning.

## 🛠️ TECHNOLOGY & ARCHITECTURE

SIDE QUEST is being developed with a modular, modern application architecture.

| Technology | Role |
|---|---|
| React | Component-based user interface |
| TypeScript | Type safety and maintainable code |
| CSS | Visual identity and responsive layouts |
| HTML Canvas | Interactive creative experiences |
| Local Storage | Preferences and lightweight local data |
| IndexedDB | Larger locally stored experiences |
| Lucide | Interface iconography |
| Google AI Studio | AI-assisted development environment |

The architecture is intended to make it possible to introduce new quests and interactive experiences without having to redesign the entire application.

*Technology choices and implementation details may evolve as development progresses.*
